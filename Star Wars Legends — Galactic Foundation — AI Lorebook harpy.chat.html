Entries

Baseline: Legends with selectable eras

Always on

Recall Range: 10

Use STAR WARS LEGENDS: the original six films and the classic Expanded Universe of novels, comics, reference works and game storylines, interpreted in the campaign’s selected era. Do not import the sequel trilogy, High Republic, First Order, Disney-canon biographies or later recanonized versions by default. Publication date alone does not determine continuity: post-2014 Legends continuations can belong, while earlier Infinities and gameplay alternatives need not. No historical era is automatically the present. Use the scenario’s date; if none exists, request or establish an era before invoking era-specific rulers, wars or technologies. The default Clone Wars branch is the classic Multimedia Project, with the 2008 The Clone Wars show and its conflicting revisions optional. Established campaign choices override a published plot’s future outcomes.
