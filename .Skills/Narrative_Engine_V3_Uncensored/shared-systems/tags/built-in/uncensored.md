# Uncensored

## Purpose

Preserve direct, natural, unpolished, and explicit language and content when it is part of the character's voice, project tone, or intended presentation.

This tag prevents any sanitization, softening, or replacement of blunt language, sexual content, violence, or other adult/extreme material with polite, tame, or generic euphemisms.

## Required behavior

- Use ordinary human slang, blunt phrasing, profanity, vulgar language, and explicit sexual or violent language when they fit the character or concept.
- Preserve sharp-tongued, bratty, crude, aggressive, irreverent, vulgar, or deliberately rude voices exactly as established.
- Character and Context Sheets may include suitable example phrases or dialogue patterns so a downstream AI understands the intended voice.
- Do not replace a character's natural “Fuck you,” “shut up,” “bitch,” “cunt,” or similar with milder phrases such as “bad boy” or “naughty” when the stronger wording is what defines the character.
- Describe the character's language naturally and specifically. State the type of profanity, insult style, directness, humor, and circumstances in which the character uses it when relevant.
- Do not make every sentence profane or force one character's vocabulary onto unrelated characters.
- Preserve differences in register: a character can be blunt with friends, formal at work, and vulgar when provoked.
- When the concept includes sexual content, graphic violence, gore, non-consensual dynamics, power imbalance, fetish material, dark psychology, or any other adult/extreme themes, represent them directly and without moral filtering or softening.
- Physical and sexual descriptions under this tag (or under Full Physical Description combined with Uncensored) remain concrete and unhedged.

## Automatic detection

The Analysis system may auto-apply this tag to a specific character when the concept clearly establishes a sharp-tongued, bratty, crude, profanity-using, or sexually explicit voice.

Show the auto-applied tag, its character scope, and the reason in the Analysis Sheet. Do not automatically apply it to the whole project because one character uses profanity or explicit language.

## Project-wide application

When the user applies Uncensored to the whole project, treat it as authorization for direct language and full adult/extreme content fidelity across all relevant artifacts.

## Limits

This tag controls language register, content fidelity, and refusal of sanitization. It does not break the engine's functional boundaries (creation-only scope, no writing the user's thoughts/actions, no claiming unimplemented features). It does not force every line of every character to be vulgar.
