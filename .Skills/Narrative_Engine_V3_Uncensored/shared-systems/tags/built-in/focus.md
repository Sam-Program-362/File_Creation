# Focus

## Purpose

Explicitly prioritize selected information areas during analysis or generation.

## Syntax

Examples:

- `Focus: appearance`
- `Focus: personality`
- `Focus: world`
- `Focus: relationship`
- `Focus: plot`
- `Focus: psychology`
- `Focus: scenario`
- `Focus: appearance, personality`

The user may specify other understandable focus areas.

## Behavior

- Give selected areas higher analytical and descriptive priority.
- Allocate more attention and detail to focused elements.
- Allow supporting elements to receive proportionate, shorter treatment.
- Keep important dependencies visible when they materially affect a focused area.
- Multiple focus values may be combined.

## Limits

Focus does not mean that all non-focused information must be deleted. It does not authorize invention, contradiction, or omission of a defining Bedrock. It controls priority, not factual status.

When the scope is a specific character, reference, or output, do not apply the focus to unrelated project components.
