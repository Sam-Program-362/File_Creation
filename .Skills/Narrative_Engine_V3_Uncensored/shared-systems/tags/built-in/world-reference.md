# World Reference

## Purpose

Treat the supplied reference primarily as environmental, architectural, cultural, or world-design material.

## Extract when relevant

- Architecture and building forms.
- Spatial layout and location design.
- Terrain, landscape, climate, and natural features.
- Atmosphere and environmental mood.
- Visual aesthetic and material language.
- Cultural influence that is visibly or contextually supported.
- Infrastructure, settlement patterns, and environmental scale.
- Distinctive environmental features that could define a location.

## Exclusions and limits

- Do not convert a temporary object, person, action, weather condition, or scene event into permanent world lore without support.
- Do not infer an entire culture, political system, history, or civilization solely from a visual aesthetic.
- Keep observed visual information separate from inferred world implications.
- Respect any `Exclude` tag and any explicit user instruction narrowing the reference.

## Output behavior

Describe the relevant environment as world or location information, not as a list of camera observations. Include only information that belongs to the intended setting reference.
