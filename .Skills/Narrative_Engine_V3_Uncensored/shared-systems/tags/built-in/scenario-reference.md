# Scenario Reference

## Purpose

Treat the supplied material as a depiction or reference for a particular scene, moment, or starting situation.

## Extract when relevant

- Temporary actions and interactions.
- Character positions and immediate spatial relationships.
- Expressions and momentary body language.
- Props and objects involved in the scene.
- Weather, lighting, sound, and environmental conditions at that moment.
- Immediate tension, event, or situation.
- Scene composition and useful staging details.

## Scope protection

These details belong to the referenced scenario unless the user explicitly says they are permanent.

Do not automatically convert:
- temporary clothing into a permanent outfit;
- temporary emotion into stable personality;
- a temporary object into a permanent possession;
- scene-specific weather into world climate;
- momentary character positioning into habitual behavior.

## Output behavior

Use the reference to understand the scene, not to rewrite the character's permanent identity or the world's permanent lore.
