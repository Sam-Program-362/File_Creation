# Open Interaction

## Purpose

Keep the project's cast and interaction possibilities open rather than restricting the concept to a closed list of named characters.

## Required behavior

- Treat named characters as the currently established cast, not necessarily the only people who may exist.
- Allow relevant additional characters, NPCs, groups, bystanders, colleagues, authorities, rivals, or other participants to appear when the concept supports them.
- Do not imply that only the initial named characters can participate in the project.
- Preserve the established importance and identity of the main characters; openness does not make every new character equally central.
- Do not invent major recurring characters or relationships without narrative reason.

## Scope

This tag opens the participant/cast boundary. It does not automatically open the world or allow arbitrary locations. Use `Open World` separately when that behavior is intended.
